Strawberry, spinach and feta salad

Prep 15 minNo cookingServes 2

Ingredients

  • 100 g baby spinach
  • 200 g strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • 60 g feta, crumbled
  • 30 g toasted almonds or walnuts
  • ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Whisk the oil, balsamic, honey and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  2. Toss the spinach and onion with half the dressing.
  3. Top with strawberries, feta and nuts and drizzle with the rest of the dressing.

Fresh tip: Add a handful of cooked quinoa or chickpeas to make it a full lunch.

Garden eggs with roasted tomatoes

Prep 10 minCook 20 minServes 2

Ingredients

  • 250 g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 courgette, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 eggs
  • Handful of spinach
  • 2 slices wholegrain toast
  • Fresh basil or chives

Method

  1. Roast the tomatoes and courgette with the oil at 200°C (400°F) for 15–20 minutes.
  2. Wilt the spinach in a pan for a minute.
  3. Poach the eggs in gently simmering water for 3–4 minutes.
  4. Pile the vegetables on toast, top with the eggs and scatter with herbs.

Fresh tip: Roast extra tomatoes; they’re lovely stirred into pasta the next day.

Fresh herb egg salad sandwich

Prep 10 minCook 10 minServes 2

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 4 slices bread
  • Lettuce and sliced cucumber

Method

  1. Boil the eggs for 9 minutes, then cool in cold water and peel.
  2. Chop and mix with yogurt, mayonnaise, mustard and herbs. Season.
  3. Layer lettuce and cucumber on the bread, spoon over the egg and close.

Fresh tip: Yogurt keeps the filling light and fresh-tasting.

Bright fruit salad with lime and mint

Prep 15 minNo cookingServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 oranges, segmented
  • 2 kiwis, sliced
  • 200 g strawberries, halved
  • 1 mango, diced
  • 150 g blueberries
  • Juice and zest of 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp honey (optional)
  • Small handful of mint, shredded

Method

  1. Prepare the fruit and combine in a large bowl.
  2. Mix the lime juice, zest and honey and pour over the fruit.
  3. Fold in the mint just before serving.

Fresh tip: Add bananas and apples just before serving so they don’t brown.

Crunchy rainbow vegetable salad

Prep 20 minNo cookingServes 4

Ingredients

  • ¼ red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 4 spring onions, sliced
  • Handful of coriander
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 2 tbsp sunflower seeds

Method

  1. Prepare all the vegetables and place in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk the oil, lemon juice and mustard with salt and pepper.
  3. Toss the salad with the dressing and sprinkle with seeds and coriander.

Fresh tip: Undressed, this salad keeps well in the fridge for two days.

Ginger vegetable noodle bowl

Prep 15 minCook 10 minServes 2

Ingredients

  • 150 g noodles
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 1 carrot, cut into thin sticks
  • 100 g sugar snap peas
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Lime wedges

Method

  1. Cook the noodles, drain and rinse.
  2. Stir-fry the pepper, carrot and peas in hot oil for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the ginger, garlic and spring onions for 1 minute.
  4. Toss in the noodles, soy sauce and sesame oil and serve with lime.

Fresh tip: Keep vegetables crisp: cook them fast over a high heat.
