Food safety and allergies

Follow safe food handling practices, keep cold dishes chilled until serving, cook minced meat to 71°C (160°F) and don’t leave perishable food out for more than two hours and store leftovers properly. Many recipes contain common allergens such as fish, shellfish, wheat, egg, dairy and nuts. Take care with boiling water and hot pans. We are not responsible for injury, illness or damage arising from cooking.

Interactive tools

The salad builder, ripeness checker and flavour pairing tool offer general guidance only. Quantities are approximate, and ripening and storage times vary with variety, temperature and how fresh produce was when bought. Cooking times and results vary with the size of your ingredients, your pans and your hob, so always check that meat and eggs are cooked through.
